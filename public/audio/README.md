# Audio sources

## apollo17/ — real Apollo 17 mission audio (NASA, public domain)

Cut from recordings published in the Apollo Flight Journal and the Apollo Lunar Surface Journal (apollojournals.org).

- `liftoff.mp3`: Kennedy launch commentary, T-10 s to tower clear (AFJ day 1 clip `a17_t-0000543.mp3`, 333.0–352.9 s)
- `landed.mp3`: "Okay, Houston. The Challenger has landed!" and CapCom's reply, GET 113:02:11 (ALSJ clip `a17a_1124856_to_1130348.mp3`, 793.3–800.5 s)

## planets/ — Gustav Holst, The Planets, Op. 32 (composed 1914–16, public domain)

Recording: Skidmore College Orchestra, provided by Musopen and released as public domain (via Wikimedia Commons, files "Gustav Holst - the planets, op. 32 - iv. jupiter, the bringer of jollity.ogg" and "... i. mars, the bringer of war.ogg").

- `jupiter-opening.mp3`: Jupiter, 0:00–0:58
- `jupiter-hymn.mp3`: Jupiter, 3:23–4:51 (central theme)
- `mars.mp3`: Mars, 0:44–1:48
