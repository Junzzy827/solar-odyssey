shuttle-pad.glb — Launch Complex 39B in the 1990s (mobile launcher platform, fixed service structure,
crew access arm with white room, oxygen vent arm with the "beanie cap", rotating service structure
parked back, water tower, lightning mast) for the STS-61 launch (src/components/shuttle/).
Source: made for this app with tools/blender/build_shuttle_pad.py (Blender bpy, procedural) and baked with
tools/blender/bake_mission.py. Dimensions from NASA Facts "Launch Complex 39 Pads A and B" and NASA KSC
"Countdown!" (2003). No third-party model or texture. meshopt compressed with tools/glb/compress.mjs.
