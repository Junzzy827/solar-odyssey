shuttle-cabin.glb (+ shuttle-cabin.atlas*.webp, shuttle-cabin.light-{day,dark}*.webp, shuttle-cabin.json)
Space Shuttle crew cabin (flight deck + middeck), STS-61 sample (src/components/shuttle/).
Source: made for this app with tools/blender/build_shuttle_cabin.py and tools/blender/shuttle_panels.py
(Blender bpy, procedural; panel faces drawn with Pillow). No third-party model or texture.
Layout and sizes: tools/shuttle/cabin.md (1988 NASA News Reference Manual sizes + estimates from
NASA photos listed in tools/shuttle/REFERENCES.md, used only to look at shape, colour and layout).
No logos, insignia, patches or flags are drawn. Panel labels are short generic codes (F7, A8, MF14K ...),
not copies of real panel text. Lighting is baked in Cycles (day / dark). meshopt compressed with tools/glb/compress.mjs.
