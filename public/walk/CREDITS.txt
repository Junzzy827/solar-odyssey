Park people (walk/mobs/*.vrm): made for Solar Odyssey in VRoid Studio using only its built-in presets
(random preset picks by a script), exported as VRM 1.0.
Motions (walk/motions.json): Mixamo (Adobe) animations, retargeted and converted to compact keyframes for these characters.

Trial park people (walk/mobs/mpfb_*.vrm): made for Solar Odyssey in Blender with MPFB 2 (MakeHuman),
using the MakeHuman system assets (CC0 1.0: body, skins, hair, clothes, eyes). Hidden body parts under
clothes removed; textures resized.
